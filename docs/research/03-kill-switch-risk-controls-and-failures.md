## 핵심 결론

- **What control categories does SEC Rule 15c3-5 (Release 34-63241) require — credit/capital thresholds, erroneous-order checks (price collars, size limits, duplicate orders), regulatory checks — and which translate to a retail bot's self-imposed limits?** — Rule 15c3-5 requires two families of control, documented, under the broker's direct and exclusive control, reviewed annually and CEO-certified [sec-15c3-5-release-34-63241][sec-15c3-5-faq]. (1) Financial controls. Pre-set credit/capital thresholds (c)(1)(i). Rejection of erroneous orders that exceed price or size parameters, judged order by order or over a short period, and of orders indicating duplicates (c)(1)(ii) [cfr-240-15c3-5]. (2) Regulatory controls. Pre-order compliance checks, entry restricted to authorized persons, and post-trade surveillance reports [sec-15c3-5-release-34-63241]. Examiner practice: a duplicate check keyed only on Order ID is deficient and must look at symbol, side, quantity and price. Price controls must keep working in illiquid sessions. Thresholds must be justifiable and soft-block overrides documented [sro-15c3-5-disciplinary-orders]. Enforcement shows the same gaps: Knight was not linked to capital thresholds [knight-capital-sec-2013], Goldman's price bands were too wide [goldman-sec-2015-133], and Citi had no hard block [fca-pra-citi-2024]. RTS 6 Art. 15 is the finer-grained version: limits per instrument and per firm, and sent orders count immediately [eu-2017-589-rts6]. Retail-bot translation. Capital threshold becomes a max notional per order, a max cumulative submitted notional per trading day, a max position per symbol and a cash cap. Erroneous-order checks become a collar against an independently fetched reference price, a max quantity, and economic-duplicate suppression (same symbol/side/qty/price within a window, in addition to ADR-0002 intent identity). Regulatory checks become market hours, VI/halt state, no-short and a symbol allow-list. Authorized persons maps to the single credential plus the human-only clear (ADR-0004 point 6). Direct and exclusive control means the limits sit at the order/risk CanSubmit edge (ADR-0004 point 1), not in the strategy.
- **Knight Capital 2012 (SEC Release 34-70694): exact control failures (dead code reactivation, no cumulative notional cap, no kill switch, 45 minutes to halt, alert emails unread) and the lessons mapped to this repo's halt/notification/deploy design.** — Knight, Aug 1 2012 [knight-capital-sec-2013][sec-pr-2013-222][wilmerhale-knight-15c3-5]. Power Peg code was retired but never removed, and was not retested after its cumulative-quantity logic moved in 2005. The new RLP feature reused Power Peg's flag. A manual deploy reached only 7 of 8 servers, with no second review. Accounts were not linked to firm-wide pre-set capital thresholds, so no cumulative cap stopped the orders. 97 automated pre-open emails were not designed or treated as alerts. More than 4M orders went out in about 45 minutes while filling 212 customer orders, more than 397M shares traded and losses exceeded $460M. Penalty: $12M plus an independent consultant. The 45-minute and 4M figures are now consistent across three pools' press-release snippets, but the PDF is still unread. The often-quoted '154 stocks' remains unverified. Repo mapping: (a) Cumulative cap. ADR-0004 point 7 defers 'unexpected loss', but a submitted-notional-per-day cap needs no P&L and is countable from ADR-0002 intents. (b) Alerts. ADR-0004 point 8 has only a notifier seam. The automatic trip is the control, and paging is only for awareness. (c) Deploy. ADR-0007 point 5 says 'as long as the deployed process always boots halted, deployment itself is harmless', and the rejected alternative 'gate prod deploy' relies on the same premise. But point 3 and point 7 only force halt on first boot or ambiguous provenance, so a redeploy of new code onto a store whose halt was already cleared boots live. That is Knight's failure mode. (d) Latency. A synchronous CanSubmit stops new exposure within one order. Knight's 45 minutes went to diagnosis.

### T3.Q3 · May 6 2010 Flash Crash (CFTC-SEC joint report) and the Aug 24 2015 ETF dislocation: what happened to market/stop orders and liquidity, and what do they imply about using MARKET orders and stop-loss logic in an unattended bot?

🟡 부분 · 우선순위 should · 확신 medium

May 6 2010 had two liquidity crises, with no fat-finger trigger [cftc-sec-flash-crash-2010]. A 75k-contract sell algorithm ran at 9% of volume with no price or time limit. Liquidity providers withdrew, market orders hit stub quotes from $0.01 to $100,000, and CME Stop Logic paused the slide. Trades more than 60% away were cancelled [waterstech-flash-crash-2010]. On Aug 24 2015 the E-Mini hit its 5% limit-down and ETPs dislocated far more than their constituents around the disorderly open. Market-wide circuit breakers did not trigger [sec-aug24-2015-research-note]. Stop-to-market conversion at depressed prices is widely asserted but was not confirmed by any pool source. Implications: an unattended bot should not send MARKET orders. Use LIMIT or bounded marketable-limit orders with a collar computed independently of the strategy. Stop logic should not fire on a single print or during opening, reopening or VI auctions, and needs fresh, sane data. Korea has no retail error-trade relief [shinkim-erroneous-trade-correction-case][krx-killswitch-error-trade-relief-2016], so bad fills stand.

### T3.Q4 · Exchange kill-switch facilities (Nasdaq Kill Switch, NYSE Risk Management Gateway, CME Kill Switch, Eurex) — what they do and whether any equivalent exists at Korean brokers/KRX or Toss for a retail API user.

🟡 부분 · 우선순위 should · 확신 medium

Nasdaq (Rule 6130, now Equity Rule 6) is optional and free. On a per-MPID net notional breach it cancels open orders and blocks entry, and re-authorization requires contacting Nasdaq operations [nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555]. NYSE Pillar has kill and reinstate actions, and only the Risk User who blocked an entity can unblock it [nyse-pillar-risk-controls]. No 'Risk Management Gateway' document was found. CME Globex cancels day and GTC orders and blocks entry, but leaves mass quotes, and does not cancel resting orders while closed or in the final 30s before the open [cme-globex-kill-switch]. Eurex has a clearing-member Stop Button whose exclusion lasts until explicitly deactivated [eurex-stop-button-2014], plus cancel-on-disconnect for non-persistent orders [tt-cancel-on-disconnect]. RTS 6 Art. 12 requires firm-side kill functionality [eu-2017-589-rts6]. The CFTC warns that a kill switch also stops risk-reducing activity [cftc-concept-release-2013]. Korea: a member-requested bulk cancel since 2016, releasable only after 10 minutes [krx-killswitch-error-trade-relief-2016][hanmag-krx-reform-etoday-2014]. Kill Switch 2.0 (exchange-initiated, effective Jan 12 2026) [krx-killswitch-2-0-2025]. Cancel-On-Disconnect for registered high-speed traders [fsc-hft-algo-registration-2023]. All of these serve exchanges, members or clearing firms. No source shows a Toss kill switch, exposure cap or cancel-on-disconnect for a retail Open API user [toss-unofficial-sdk], and the official spec was not checked in this round. internal/killswitch is the bot's only kill switch.

### T3.Q5 · KRX and FSS rules on algorithmic/erroneous-order prevention: 착오주문 방지, 호가 제한, 대량주문 사전확인, broker-side 주문 적정성 점검, and KRX 'error trade' cancellation (착오매매 구제) procedures.

🟡 부분 · 우선순위 must · 확신 medium

KRX equities. Art. 65(2) requires a quote suitability check (account, ticker, suspended status). From Sep 2014 a single quote is capped at 1% of listed shares (previously 5%, tiered) and over-cap quotes are rejected [kcmi-krx-rule-updates-order-limits]. KRX derivatives. Real-time price limits from Sep 2014 reject out-of-band quotes. The application is due within 30 minutes, KRX decides by 17:00 the next day, and single-price fills are excluded. A member-requested kill switch was added at the same time [krx-killswitch-error-trade-relief-2016]. Correction is limited to the member's own error [shinkim-erroneous-trade-correction-case]. Courts treat input errors as gross negligence, and the trades stand [hanmag-supreme-court-2023][seoul-high-court-2015na2055371]. FSC/FSS: high-speed algo traders must register, and their brokers must pre-check their order systems (2023) [fsc-hft-algo-registration-2023]. Keep bands tight [goldman-sec-2015-133], and make sure they work in illiquid sessions [sro-15c3-5-disciplinary-orders]. Enforce an aggregate quota [everbright-csrc-2013]. The collar, max quantity and stale-data guard are per-order rejects that do not latch. Unlike Freqtrade, a time-based auto-unlock should not apply to the global halt.

### T3.Q8 · Design patterns for human-cleared halts: operator clear CLI/CLI-signing, two-person rule, clear reason logging, 'arm' vs 'authorize' semantics, and how to prevent an automation from clearing (ADR-0004 point 6, #80).

🟡 부분 · 우선순위 must · 확신 low

No direct literature was found on operator-clear CLIs or two-person reinstatement. Precedents. Nasdaq requires a manual re-authorization through operations [nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555]. NYSE lets only the blocking Risk User unblock [nyse-pillar-risk-controls]. The Eurex Stop Button persists until an explicit deactivation [eurex-stop-button-2014]. KRX allows release only after a minimum of 10 minutes [krx-killswitch-error-trade-relief-2016]. RTS 6 requires a documented override procedure and order attribution [eu-2017-589-rts6], and SRO orders require documented justification for overrides [sro-15c3-5-disciplinary-orders]. Counter-examples: Samsung's maker-checker was a formality [samsung-securities-fss-2018] and Citi's warnings were clicked through [fca-pra-citi-2024]. Freqtrade's auto-expiring locks [freqtrade-protections] are the design to avoid for a global halt. Proposed design for #80. (1) A separate cmd/ operator binary, with a test asserting that the cmd/bot process cannot reach the global clear (consistent with ADR-0014 point 5). (2) The clear names the halt it clears (reason plus generation or trip time) as a compare-and-swap, so a re-trip after the operator looked is not silently cleared. (3) Operator identity and reason are made durable in the audit sink before the cleared state is visible (ADR-0006/0012). (4) The CLI shows the evidence (counters, ambiguous backlog, recent audit events) and asks for typed confirmation. (5) Keep authorize and clear as one primitive (ADR-0007), optionally bound to a build or config hash. (6) An optional minimum hold. (7) List it as sacred under the gate-promotion rule.

### T3.Q9 · Dead-man / watchdog interaction with kill switches: what should trip when the bot itself is unhealthy (no heartbeat, clock skew, store down) — relation to ADR-0012 store-down fail-closed.

🟡 부분 · 우선순위 should · 확신 low

Sources: FIA covers failure detection, emergency notification and market-data reasonability [fia-automated-trading-guide-2015][fia-automated-trading-risk-controls-2024]. Cancel-on-disconnect exists at KRX (members, high-speed traders) and Eurex [fsc-hft-algo-registration-2023][tt-cancel-on-disconnect]. RTS 25 treats clock synchronisation as a regulated health condition (1ms for non-HFT algo trading) [pico-rts25-clock]. Synthesis: an unhealthy bot cannot rescue itself, so health checks must fail closed in-process. Existing coverage: a store error blocks (ADR-0012), and replay blocks until restore completes (ADR-0004 point 3). Proposed additions: (a) a stale market-data or holdings guard; (b) a clock-sanity guard on wall-versus-monotonic divergence or an unconfirmed calendar date, since the ADR-0014 settle window and the 15m token window depend on time; (c) the Supervisor trips the global halt when the reconciler or ADR-0014 ticker stops heart-beating, rather than only restarting it. Externally there is no Toss cancel-on-disconnect, so resting orders survive a crash, and the runbook needs a manual cancel-all. Rotating client_secret is the reliable external stop: refresh failures then trip the persisted halt (3 per 15m).

### T3.Q10 · Circuit-breaker pattern (Nygard, Release It!; Hystrix/resilience4j semantics) vs trading kill switch: half-open probing is unsafe for order submission — what literature supports 'never auto-close' for money-moving breakers?

Nygard/Fowler half-open auto-probes with a live trial call [fowler-circuit-breaker]. Resilience4j allows about 10 probes against Hystrix's 1, can transition automatically, and can hang in half-open [resilience4j-half-open]. A probe is safe only when the server guarantees idempotent replay, as Stripe does (stored first response replayed, parameter mismatch rejected) [stripe-idempotency]. ### T3.Q11 · Market-wide circuit breakers and per-stock VI/LULD: how should a bot behave during a halt (cancel resting orders? pause? avoid re-entry at reopen auction) and how to detect halts via the API.

🟡 부분 · 우선순위 should · 확신 low

US: market-wide circuit breakers at 7%, 13% and 20% (15-minute halt; Level 3 ends the day) and LULD Tier 1 at 5% [sec-luld-circuit-breaker-2012]. KRX: circuit breakers at 8%, 15% and 20% (20-minute halt; Level 3 ends the session) and a sidecar for program quotes [krx-circuit-breaker-news-2026]. VI: dynamic about 3%/6%, static about 10%, then a 2-minute auction. Nextrade reportedly accepts only cancels during VI [krx-vi-kbthink]. These figures are non-official. CME does not cancel resting orders while closed or just before the open [cme-globex-kill-switch]. Reopen auctions are where prices dislocate [sec-aug24-2015-research-note]. Recommended behaviour: VI means a per-symbol no-new-exposure block, a market-wide breaker means a global block, and stop triggers are suppressed during auctions. Do not cancel resting limits by default, because a cancel during a halt may be rejected or come back ambiguous and feed the ADR-0014 backlog. Re-enter only after continuous trading resumes plus a cool-down, with the collar re-anchored. API detection is unverified: check the official OpenAPI spec for a halt or VI status field and for rejection codes, and fall back to market-calendar session state.

## 프로젝트 함의

- ADR-0004 point 1 (a synchronous CanSubmit edge plus a fail-closed re-check before submit-attempted) matches RTS 6 Art. 15 and 15c3-5 'direct and exclusive control' [eu-2017-589-rts6][cfr-240-15c3-5]. Notional, collar and quantity limits belong at this edge in order/risk, not in internal/strategy (still doc.go only).
- ADR-0004 point 7 defers 'unexpected loss' for lack of orderId-linked P&L. Knight's gap was a missing cumulative exposure cap, not P&L [knight-capital-sec-2013]. A submitted-notional-per-trading-day cap counted from ADR-0002 intents is journal-reconstructible and should exist before a strategy is attached.
- ADR-0002 dedup is intent-identity based, with clientOrderId as the server-side hint, and the spec leaves duplicate clientOrderId behavior undefined. SRO orders found Order-ID-only dedup deficient and expect checks on symbol, side, quantity and price [sro-15c3-5-disciplinary-orders]. A strategy bug that emits fresh intents for the same economic order passes ADR-0002, so add an economic-duplicate window check at the risk edge.
- The env thresholds (order failures 3, token refresh 3 per 15m, ambiguous backlog 3) are structural only. Add an orders-per-window rate cap that trips the global halt, given Everbright's ~26k orders in 2s and Hanmag's ~41k in ~2 minutes [everbright-csrc-2013][hanmag-supreme-court-2023].
- FIA 'block if no size limit configured' [fia-automated-trading-risk-controls-2024] matches env.example's rule that a threshold must be > 0 or the bot refuses to start. Extend it to per-symbol and per-order notional: an absent limit means blocked.
- Compute the price collar from independently fetched internal/market reference data and make it a hard reject. Hanmag's bound died with the same computation, Goldman's band was too wide, and Citi's soft warnings were dismissed [hanmag-krx-reform-etoday-2014][goldman-sec-2015-133][fca-pra-citi-2024].
- Make LIMIT-only (or bounded marketable-limit) a rule in the order layer, given the flash-crash stub fills and the disorderly Aug 2015 open [waterstech-flash-crash-2010][sec-aug24-2015-research-note].
- ADR-0004 point 2 (the halt still allows exposure-reducing cleanup) is supported by the CFTC [cftc-concept-release-2013], but exchange and RTS 6 kills also cancel resting orders [nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555][eu-2017-589-rts6]. Add an operator cancel-all-unexecuted action next to #80, because Toss provides none.
- ADR-0004 point 6 and ADR-0014 point 5 (human-only global clear) align with Nasdaq, NYSE, Eurex and KRX reinstatement practice [nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555][nyse-pillar-risk-controls][eurex-stop-button-2014][krx-killswitch-error-trade-relief-2016]. Proposed for #80: a separate cmd/ binary; a compare-and-swap clear on the halt generation; identity and reason made durable before the clear is visible (ADR-0012); a test that cmd/bot cannot clear; and sacred listing (CODEOWNERS + sacredRequiredPaths + a fail-closed test).
- ADR-0007 point 5 (verified text: 'as long as the deployed process always boots halted, deployment itself is harmless') and the rejected 'prod deploy gate' alternative rest on a premise that points 3 and 7 guarantee only for first boot or ambiguous provenance. A redeploy onto a cleared store boots live, which is Knight's failure mode [knight-capital-sec-2013]. Consider an amendment binding the clear record to a build or config hash, reusing the same halt/clear primitive (ADR-0007 rejects a second authorize flag).
- ADR-0004 point 8 has a notifier seam, and CLAUDE.md says external alerting is undecided. Knight's unreviewed emails show that alerts are not a control, but go-live with a strategy should require a decided paging channel, because a halted bot with resting orders that nobody notices is a silent outage.
- ADR-0012 store-down fail-closed is the in-process dead-man for the store. Remaining gaps: stale data, clock sanity (ADR-0014 settle window, 15m token window) and silent load-bearing goroutines. The Supervisor should trip the global halt, not just restart, when the reconciler or ticker heartbeat stops. Toss has no cancel-on-disconnect [fsc-hft-algo-registration-2023][tt-cancel-on-disconnect].
- Under ADR-0001's one valid token per client, an external token reissue races the bot's own refresh. Rotating client_secret is the reliable external stop, via the token-refresh trip (3 per 15m). Document it in docs/runbooks/.
- ADR-0003/0014 (no auto-resubmit, backlog escalation) are validated by Everbright's resend-on-timeout [everbright-csrc-2013] and by the Stripe contrast: retries are safe only under a server-guaranteed idempotent replay, which the Toss spec does not define [stripe-idempotency].
- Add VI, circuit-breaker and sidecar awareness on top of the market calendar: a per-symbol block during VI, a global block during a breaker, stop suppression in auctions and a re-anchored collar after the reopen. API detection is unverified.
- Korean relief is member-only and needs at least 10bn KRW of loss [shinkim-erroneous-trade-correction-case][krx-killswitch-error-trade-relief-2016], so erroneous fills are irreversible. Use conservative default limits at the first ADR-0007 authorization.
- Testing: add an e2e assertion that every trip path yields zero further POST /api/v1/orders while reads and the reconciler continue (httptest, -race), before a strategy is attached.

## 출처 간 상충

- Citi date: the task says Dec 2022, but the FCA and PRA say 2 May 2022 [fca-pra-citi-2024]. The $444bn/$255bn/$189bn/$1.4bn figures are successive layers, not a conflict.
- Hanmag loss: 46.2bn KRW, 460억 and 463억 agree. One pool's '452-463 billion KRW' is a unit error. 41,336 orders and ~37k fills measure different things. Causes are reported as a system error, a parameter error or days/0, and the court-era days/0 account is the most specific [hanmag-supreme-court-2023].
- Knight: '4M+ orders in 45 min' (press release snippets) and '397M shares' are different units. Losses are quoted as $440M to $460M+, so use 'over $460M' [knight-capital-sec-2013][sec-pr-2013-222].
- Samsung: 16 employees sold versus 21 referred to prosecutors, which are different populations. The 37 minutes counts from recognition, not from the input. '30x' and '31x' issued shares agree [samsung-securities-fss-2018].
- Nasdaq kill switch: 'post-trade best efforts' and 'pre-set notional block' are compatible, because exposure is computed post-trade. Rule 6130 and Equity Rule 6 are old and current numbering [nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555].
- KRX static VI is described as 'new in 2026' by one snippet, but it has existed since about 2015 (general knowledge), so the snippet likely conflates KRX with Nextrade [krx-vi-kbthink].
- The 15c3-5 FAQ date is given as 2010, 2011, 2012 or 2017. Most likely it was first issued around 2011 and last updated Oct 12 2017 [sec-15c3-5-faq].
- Everbright penalty basis: the glitch versus pre-disclosure hedging. The penalty attached to the hedging, while the CSRC also cited design and control failures [everbright-csrc-2013].
- Freqtrade's auto-expiring locks [freqtrade-protections] conflict with the exchange and regulator norm of explicit reinstatement [nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555][eurex-stop-button-2014]. Resolve by using time-based reset only for non-latching per-symbol cool-downs and never for the global halt.
- Internal tension: ADR-0007 point 5 assumes the process 'always boots halted', but points 3 and 7 force halt only on first boot or ambiguous provenance.

### 출처 요약

- **`sec-15c3-5-release-34-63241`** — Adopting release for Rule 15c3-5 (adopted Nov 3 2010, compliance July 14 2011), which ended naked market access. Broker-dealers must keep documented financial controls (pre-set credit/capital thresholds, rejection of erroneous orders) and regulatory controls (pre-order compliance checks, entry limited to authorized persons, post-trade surveillance) under their direct and exclusive control. Controls are reviewed annually and the CEO certifies. This is the reference control set for the bot's self-imposed limits. No pool fetched the PDF. _(접근: open access; not fetched (sec.gov blocked); search snippets)_
- **`cfr-240-15c3-5`** — Codified rule text. Under (c)(1)(ii) the firm must reject orders that exceed price or size parameters, judged order by order or over a short period, and orders that indicate duplicates. Under (c)(1)(i) it must keep pre-set credit/capital thresholds. These map directly to a price collar, max order size, rolling notional cap and duplicate suppression. _(접근: open access; seen in search listing only)_
- **`sec-15c3-5-faq`** — Staff FAQ. It says pre-trade prevention of erroneous activity is the rule's primary focus and that market-maker quotes are not excluded. A broker may use third-party or exchange tools to satisfy the controls but stays responsible. Search results give a last update of Oct 12 2017. _(접근: open access; not fetched)_
- **`sro-15c3-5-disciplinary-orders`** — Exchange enforcement orders that show how examiners test 15c3-5. A duplicate-order control keyed only on Order ID was held deficient; the check should cover symbol, side, quantity and price. Price-deviation controls that stopped working in illiquid pre- and post-market sessions were deficient. Soft-block overrides need documented justification, and firms must be able to justify their thresholds. Several related orders were merged in the search summary. _(접근: open access; search-summary level, details span several orders)_
- **`knight-capital-sec-2013`** — Settled order for the Aug 1 2012 SMARS failure and the first SEC action under 15c3-5. Causes: the Power Peg code was retired but never removed, and was not retested after its cumulative-quantity logic moved in 2005. A new RLP feature reused Power Peg's flag. A manual deploy missed 1 of 8 servers, with no second-technician review. Accounts were not linked to firm-wide capital thresholds. 97 automated pre-open emails were not treated as alerts. The router sent more than 4M orders in about 45 minutes while trying to fill 212 customer orders, traded more than 397M shares and lost more than $460M. Knight paid a $12M penalty and had to hire an independent consultant. _(접근: open access; sec.gov blocked; mirror https://contracts.justia.com/companies/kcg-holdings-inc-18985/contract/555902)_
- **`sec-pr-2013-222`** — Official announcement. Three round-2 pools' snippets agree on the core figures: more than 4M orders in 45 minutes against 212 customer orders, 97 pre-open emails and a $12M penalty. These figures are therefore upgraded from 'secondary' to snippet-level official. _(접근: open access; not fetched)_
- **`wilmerhale-knight-15c3-5`** — Law-firm reading of the order. The emails were not designed as system alerts and were generally not reviewed. Controls lacked proper design and testing. _(접근: open access; snippet)_
- **`cftc-sec-flash-crash-2010`** — Joint staff report (Sep 30 2010). It describes two liquidity crises, in E-Mini futures and in single stocks, and found no fat-finger trigger. Trades more than 60% from reference prices were cancelled. The body was not read. _(접근: open access; not fetched)_
- **`waterstech-flash-crash-2010`** — Mechanism-level summary of the joint report. A 75k-contract sell algorithm ran at 9% of volume with no price or time limit. Liquidity providers withdrew, stub quotes from $0.01 to $100,000 executed, and CME Stop Logic paused trading. _(접근: open access; secondary)_
- **`sec-aug24-2015-research-note`** — Data-only note (Dec 2015). The E-Mini hit its 5% limit-down. ETPs dislocated more than corporate stocks around a disorderly open. Reg SHO restrictions triggered in more than 2,000 securities. Market-wide circuit breakers did not trigger. _(접근: open access; not fetched)_
- **`nasdaq-kill-switch-34-71555`** — Optional, free kill switch effective Mar 1 2014 (Rule 6130). When a participant-set per-MPID net notional threshold is breached, Nasdaq cancels open orders and blocks new ones. The participant must contact Nasdaq operations to re-authorize. It supplements, and does not replace, the firm's own controls. _(접근: open access; not fetched; filing text https://listingcenter.nasdaqomx.com/assets/rulebook/nasdaq/filings/SR-NASDAQ-2014-017.pdf)_
- **`nyse-pillar-risk-controls`** — Designated Risk Users take kill and reinstate actions. Only the Risk User who blocked an entity can unblock it, so clear authority is bound to the block. _(접근: open access; snippet)_
- **`cme-globex-kill-switch`** — Cancels day and GTC orders and blocks new entry. It leaves mass quotes in place, and does not cancel resting orders while the market is closed or in the final 30 seconds before the open. _(접근: open access; snippet)_
- **`eurex-stop-button-2014`** — A clearing member's Stop Button excludes a non-clearing member from trading. The exclusion lasts until a new system entry deactivates it, so there is no automatic reset, and each case is documented. This is a clearing-credit kill rather than a retail control. T7 technical documentation was not found. _(접근: German-only; snippet)_
- **`tt-cancel-on-disconnect`** — Vendor table of exchanges that support cancel-on-disconnect; for example, Eurex supports it for non-persistent orders. It is a session-level dead-man facility that REST retail APIs lack. _(접근: open access; snippet)_
- **`eu-2017-589-rts6`** — Art. 12 requires kill functionality that immediately cancels any or all unexecuted orders. Art. 15 requires pre-trade controls: price collars, maximum order value and volume, message limits, and market and credit limits, applied per client, instrument, trader, desk and firm. Sent orders count toward limits immediately. Overriding a blocked order needs a documented procedure, and every order must be attributable to an algorithm and a responsible person. _(접근: open access; UK copy https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/589/chapter/II/section/3)_
- **`fia-automated-trading-guide-2015`** — An industry framework rather than a cookbook. Pre-trade: maximum order size, price collars, message throttles. Operations: failure detection, emergency notification, change management, testing. _(접근: open access; excerpts)_
- **`fia-automated-trading-risk-controls-2024`** — Lists maximum order size, intraday position, market-data reasonability, price tolerance and repeated-execution limits. It recommends blocking orders where no size limit is configured, a fail-closed default. _(접근: open access; snippets)_
- **`cftc-concept-release-2013`** — Groups controls into pre-trade, post-trade, system safeguards and other protections. - **`freqtrade-protections`** — Built-in loss limits of an open-source retail bot. The shared parameters are lookback_period, trade_limit and stop_duration, and locks auto-expire.
